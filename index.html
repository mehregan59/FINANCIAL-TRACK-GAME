PHYSICAL BOARD CRAFTING KIT (4 SHEETS)

Print these 4 separate sheets to cut out and assemble a physical 50cm rotating board game using a brass split pin (paper fastener).

SHEET 1 OF 4: MAIN BOARD TRACK (1 TO 100) SCALE: 50cm FULL BOARD BASE
SHEET 2 OF 4: ROTATING NUMBER DIAL RINGS (0 - 9) CUT OUT EACH RING ALONG DASHED LINES
SHEET 3 OF 4: COVER SHIELD PLATES (WITH WINDOW APERTURE CUTOUTS) CUT OUT RECTANGULAR WINDOW BOXES AT TOP
SHEET 4 OF 4: ASSEMBLY DIAGRAM & STANDUP PAWN TOKENS PHYSICAL GAME ACCESSORIES

ASSEMBLY STEPS:

  1. Cut out the main board circle on Sheet 1.
  2. Cut out the 3 number dial rings on Sheet 2.
  3. Cut out the 3 cover shield plates on Sheet 3 and slice open the marked rectangular window aperture boxes at 12 o'clock.
  4. Stack layers from bottom to top:
    [Main Board] → [Hundreds Dial] → [Hundreds Cover] → [Tens Dial] → [Tens Cover] → [Units Dial] → [Units Cover]
  5. Push a single brass paper fastener pin (split pin) through the center hole (+) to lock all layers together so dials spin freely!

How a round works

The same rhythm as real investing: the market moves, events hit, you decide, you check your wealth.

1
🎲

Roll & move

Tap the dice, press Accept and your avatar hops space by space around the market board. Roll a 6 and you go again.

2
📰

Read the market

Every space carries a market event. Bull runs, crashes and dividends push the Market Tracker up or down.

3
🏦

Buy, hold or sell

Buy shares when prices are low, sell to lock in gains, or hold your nerve through the dip.

4
📊

Check your wealth

Money plus shares at the current price is your total. Watch it climb and fall with the market.

Strategy. Risk. Reward.

Three ideas every investor lives by, and you feel all three within one game.

Strategy

  • Time your decisions
  • Build your portfolio
  • Respond to market events

Risk

  • Weigh losses before acting
  • Manage limited money
  • Adapt to sudden shifts

Reward

  • Gains from rising prices
  • Well-timed decisions pay off
  • Patience beats panic

Spot the feelings behind the trades. Fear, overconfidence, herd behaviour and panic selling show up at the table, just like in real markets, only this time nothing real is at stake.

Capital Clash is a learning game. It is not financial advice.
