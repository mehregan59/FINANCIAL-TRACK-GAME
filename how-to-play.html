The game in one minute

How a game flows

Roll, move, land on a tile that moves the market, then trade shares before you pass the dice. The turn order never changes.

Create or join

The host creates a room and shares the code or invite link. Everyone picks a different avatar.

Pick a length

The host chooses Long, Standard, Short or Beginner in the lobby, then presses Start.

Roll and move

Tap the dice, press Accept, and your pawn hops as many spaces as you rolled.

Tile moves the market

Plus tiles raise the Market Tracker, minus tiles lower it, Skip changes nothing.

Trade at the Bank

Buy or sell shares at the new Market Tracker value.

End your turn

Press End turn. The first pawn on space 100 starts the final round.

Every step is manual. Only the player whose turn it is can press the buttons, and the control you need glows. When you press End turn, the market takes its forecast step.

The heart of the game

The board and the Market Tracker

The board is a circle of 100 spaces, each its own bar with a clear gap between bars. Every bar shows its number (yellow), its name, and the effect in bold at the end, for example "Bubble Eruption -18". The Market Tracker value in the middle is the current market price, between 0 and 999, and it starts at 500.

Crypto Rally (+14)Bear Market (-9)Supply Halt (Skip)
  • Plus tile: the number is added to the Market Tracker.
  • Minus tile: the number is subtracted.
  • Skip tile: no change.
  • Limits: tiles can never take the value below 0 or above 999.
  • Magnifier: press the Magnifier button in the strip above the board, then move your finger or mouse over the board. A round lens follows you and shows that spot bigger, turned so the text reads left to right. It only changes your own screen. While an avatar hops, every screen zooms on it automatically and holds the landing tile for a few seconds.
  • Hands off: players cannot change the tracker by hand. Only tiles and the market forecast move it, and the host can reset it.
  • No built-in drift: on every board the plus tiles and the minus tiles add up to the same total, so the market is as likely to fall as to rise.
  • Reshuffled road: after every turn the tiles ahead of the pawns are shuffled.

You land on Crypto Rally (+14) while the market is at 500.

The mood of the market

Forecasts: Bull, Bear and Neutral

A phase is a forecast, not a promise. A big pop-up announces it, the bar above the board shows it, and like a real market it can change course.

A calm market. Only tiles move the price.

▲

The market looks set to rise. The price creeps up turn by turn.

Plus tiles count 1 extra: +3 becomes +4
▼

Bear forecast

The market looks set to fall. The price creeps down turn by turn.

How a forecast plays out

Announcement

A pop-up says the forecast and reminds you it is not a promise.

Small steps

When each turn ends, the market takes a small step, and some steps are zero. A Bull or Bear phase adds up to 5% to 20% of the value at its start (Beginner: 3% to 10%).

Sudden change

In about 3 of 10 Bull or Bear phases the forecast changes after the first real step. The rest goes the other way with the amount that was left (7 times in 10), or stops (3 times in 10).

Calm can break

A Neutral phase can also turn into Bull or Bear at any moment, with a fresh total.

Example: a Bull forecast that turns

The market rises from 500 to 517, then the forecast reverses and it ends at 484.

Safety net. Forecast steps never push the market below 50 or above 950, so it always has room to move both ways. Tiles can still use the full range from 0 to 999.
Buy low, sell high

The Bank

The Bank opens for you after you have moved and your tile has been applied. The share price equals the current Market Tracker value, so a share costs more when the market is high.

  • Buy: press Buy, enter how many shares in the window that opens, and confirm. You need enough money.
  • Sell: press Sell, enter how many shares and confirm. You need enough shares.
  • Prices move: shares are valued at the updated tracker, so your total changes even when you do not trade.

Who is ahead

Ranking and the changing board

When a turn ends, the players list is sorted from the highest total value to the lowest, and each player gets a rank number. #1 is the richest. The ranking is for display only: the turn order never changes.

After each turn the tiles ahead of the pawns are reshuffled, so you cannot plan your whole route from the tile texts you see now.

The finish

Ending the game

1. Final round

The first pawn to reach space 100 starts the final round, announced by a pop-up.

2. One last turn

Every player who has not yet played in the current round gets one last turn, including the Bank.

3. Results

The results screen ranks everyone by total value.

4. Winner

The highest total wins. If two players have exactly the same total, they share the win.

Reaching 100 first does not win by itself; it only ends the game. A big portfolio beats a fast pawn.

Good to know

Helpers

Turn pop-up and glow

When a turn starts, a pop-up shows whose turn it is on every screen. The control you must use glows: the dice, then Accept, then the Bank. End turn is locked for 5 seconds after your move; when it unlocks, End turn glows instead of the Bank.

Magnifier

The Magnifier button above the board gives you a round lens that follows your finger or mouse and makes the tiles readable. It is also used automatically while a pawn moves.

Guided tour

When you start a new room you can choose a guided tour. It points at each part of the screen with a short explanation and shows only the first time. In Beginner games it is always on.

Save and resume

The game is saved automatically and kept for 24 hours. If someone disconnects they can come back and keep playing, and a saved game can be resumed instead of starting over.

Reset

Only the host can reset, after a confirm dialog. The Market Tracker returns to its start value (500 by default), every pawn goes back to space 1, and everyone gets their starting money and shares back.

Event cards

The card slot next to the Bank is reserved for market events. It is marked "coming soon" for now.
