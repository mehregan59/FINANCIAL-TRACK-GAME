Your game screen

This is what you see during a game. Press Next to walk through each part of the screen.

  1. Top bar: whose turn it is, the Forecast, the Magnifier, font choice, How to play, Tour and Home.
  2. Player Portfolio (left): each player’s Tile, shares, cash and total value.
  3. Space Board (centre): 100 Tiles, the Start Tile 0 at the top right, and the share price in the middle.
  4. Dice (right, top): roll, then press Accept.
  5. The Bank (right): share price, previous price, Buy and Sell.
  6. Event Card Deck (right, bottom): glows when you land on a gold Tile.
Know your table

Game Components

These are the parts of the game screen. Their names are always written in this colour in the guide.

1Dice

Tap it on your turn. The value you roll is how many Tiles your Player Avatar moves. Long mode uses a die with value 1–6.

2Space Board

The big ring made of 100 Tiles, numbered 1 to 100. Every Tile shows its number, a market event and its effect.

3Market Tracker

The share price in the middle of the Space Board (0 to 999, starts at 500). The Tile you land on moves it up (+) or down (−).

4The Bank

Opens after you move. Buy shares from The Bank or sell shares to it, always at the Market Tracker price.

5Player Portfolio

Every investor’s Tile, shares, cash and total value, sorted from richest to poorest.

6Event Card Deck

10 Event Cards. Land on a gold Event Card Tile, tap the deck and the top card turns over. Read what it means for you, then press Accept Event.

7Forecast bar

The strip above the Space Board. It is not a Tile: it shows the mood of the market (Bull, Bear or Neutral).

The game in one minute

How a game flows

Roll the Dice, move, land on a Tile that moves the Market Tracker, then trade shares at The Bank before you pass the Dice. The turn order never changes.

Create or join

The host creates a room and shares the code or invite link. Each player picks an avatar of their choice. Avatars already taken by another player are greyed out and cannot be picked.

Pick a length

The host chooses Long, Standard, Short or Beginner in the lobby, then presses Start.

Roll and move

Tap the Dice, press Accept, and your Player Avatar hops the Dice value and lands on a Tile on the Space Board.

Tile moves the market

Plus Tiles raise the Market Tracker, minus Tiles lower it, Skip Tiles change nothing.

Trade at The Bank

Buy or sell shares at the new Market Tracker price.

End your Turn

Press End turn. The first Player Avatar on Tile 100 starts the final round.

Why the Dice change. In Short, Standard and Beginner the Dice have bigger values so players can reach Tile 100 in fewer Turns. The 10-sided die has one spare face (10) and the 20-sided die has two (19 and 20); they are never rolled. After the die lands, your value is shown big and red next to it.

Step by step

Your Turn

Every step is manual. Only the player whose Turn it is can press the buttons, and the control you need glows. When you press End turn, the market takes its Forecast step.

Roll

Tap the Dice. It tumbles and lands.

Tap the Dice

Accept

Check your value and confirm the move.

Accept: move 5

Move

Your Player Avatar hops forward one Tile at a time.

Tile

The market movement on your landed Tile on the Space Board is applied to the Market Tracker.

The Bank

The Bank opens and glows. Buy or sell shares, or skip it.

End turn

The 5s safety wait: End turn stays locked for 5 seconds (with a countdown) after your move, so nobody ends a Turn by accident before using The Bank. Buying or selling unlocks it at once.

End turn (5)

A player who has already reached Tile 100 does not roll again.

The heart of the game

The Space Board and the Market Tracker

The Space Board is a circle of 100 Tiles. Every Tile shows its number in a white circle, its name and its effect, for example “Bubble Eruption −18”. Tiles have only three colours: green for plus Tiles, red for minus Tiles and gold for Event Card Tiles. The number in the middle of the Space Board is the current share price, between 0 and 999, and it starts at 500.

Crypto Rally (+14)Bear Market (-9)Supply Halt (Skip)
  • Plus Tile (+1, +2, +3 and so on): the number is added to the Market Tracker.
  • Minus Tile (−1, −2, −3 and so on): the number is subtracted.
  • Skip Tile: no change.
  • Limits: Tiles can never take the price below 0 or above 999.
  • Magnifier: press the Magnifier button in the strip above the Space Board, then move your finger or mouse over it. A round lens shows that spot bigger. While a Player Avatar hops, every screen zooms on it automatically.
  • Hands off: players cannot change the Market Tracker by hand. Only Tiles and the Forecast move it, and the host can reset it.
  • No built-in drift: all plus Tiles together add up to exactly the same amount as all minus Tiles together, so the market is as likely to fall as to rise.
  • Dynamic Tile Shuffling: after every Turn, the Tiles that no Player Avatar has reached yet swap places at random. Like real news, you cannot plan your whole route ahead.
Example

You land on Crypto Rally (+14) while the Market Tracker is at 500.

500 + 14 = 514

The next player lands on Bear Market (−9).

514 − 9 = 505

The mood of the market

Forecasts: Bull, Bear and Neutral

The Forecast is not a Tile or a card. It is the general mood of the market, shown in the bar above the Space Board. A big pop-up announces each new Forecast, and like a real market it can change course.

The market looks set to fall. The price creeps down Turn by Turn.

How a Forecast plays out

Announcement

A pop-up says the Forecast and reminds you it is not a promise.

Small steps

When each Turn ends, the Market Tracker takes a small step, and some steps are zero. A Bull or Bear phase adds up to 5% to 20% of the price at its start (Beginner: 3% to 10%).

Sudden change

In about 3 of 10 Bull or Bear phases the Forecast changes after the first real step. The rest goes the other way (7 times in 10), or stops (3 times in 10).

Calm can break

A Neutral phase can also turn into Bull or Bear at any moment.

Example: a Bull Forecast that turns

The Market Tracker rises from 500 to 517, then the Forecast reverses and it ends at 484.

Safety net. Forecast steps never push the Market Tracker below 50 or above 950, so it always has room to move both ways. Tiles can still use the full range from 0 to 999.
Surprise!

The Event Card Deck

10 of the 100 Tiles are gold Event Card Tiles. Any two of them are always at least 7 Tiles apart, also after Dynamic Tile Shuffling. Land on one and the Event Card Deck glows with an arrow: tap it, the top card turns over in the middle of the screen and shows its name, its rule and exactly what it means for you (with your own cash and shares). Press Accept Event, then The Bank opens as usual. When all 10 cards are used the deck is reshuffled.

Penalty: if you cannot fulfil a card (not enough cash or shares), nothing is paid or traded, but you must skip your next turn.

Buy low, sell high

The Bank

The Bank opens for you after you have moved and your Tile has been applied. The share price equals the current Market Tracker value, so a share costs more when the market is high.

  • Enter The Bank: press the one Enter The Bank button. A small window opens on top of The Bank.
  • Buy: choose the Buy tab, enter how many shares (or press Max) and confirm. You need enough cash.
  • Sell: choose the Sell tab, enter how many shares (or press Max) and confirm. You need enough shares.
  • After an Event Card: The Bank stays closed until your next Turn.
  • Prices move: your shares are valued at the latest Market Tracker price, so your total changes even when you do not trade.

Who is ahead

Ranking

When a Turn ends, the Player Portfolio are sorted from the highest total value to the lowest, and each player gets a rank number. #1 is the richest. The ranking is for display only: the turn order never changes.

Remember Dynamic Tile Shuffling: the Tiles ahead of the Player Avatars change places after each Turn.

The finish

Ending the game

1. Final round

The first Player Avatar to reach Tile 100 starts the final round, announced by a pop-up.

2. One last Turn

Every player who has not yet played in the current round gets one last Turn, including The Bank.

3. Results

The results screen ranks everyone by total value.

4. Winner

The highest total wins. If two players have exactly the same total, they share the win.

Reaching Tile 100 first does not win by itself; it only ends the game. A big portfolio beats a fast Player Avatar.

Good to know

Helpers

Turn pop-up and glow

When a Turn starts, a pop-up shows whose Turn it is on every screen. The control you must use glows: the Dice, then Accept, then The Bank, then End turn.

Magnifier

The Magnifier button above the Space Board gives you a round lens that follows your finger or mouse and makes the Tiles readable.

Tour Guide button

The Tour Guide button at the top of the game screen opens a menu: How to play (this guide and the Summary), the Screen tour (step by step, with Back), and Report a bug.

Game chat

In a live room, the round chat button at the bottom right opens the chat. Write messages, add emoji and close it again any time. A red number shows new messages.

Notify a player

Waiting for someone? Press Notify in the chat: their screen plays a sound and shows a reminder that it is their Turn.

Remove a player who does not play

If one Turn takes longer than 3 minutes, the Vote to remove button in the chat opens. When all other players vote, that player is removed from the game and play goes on.

Language and font

Under the game name you choose the Font and the Language (English or Deutsch). Each player picks their own; it is remembered on that device.

Report a bug

Something went wrong? Open Tour Guide, then Report a bug, describe what happened and send it.

Save and resume

The game is saved automatically and kept for 24 hours. If someone disconnects they can come back and keep playing.

Reset

Only the host can reset, after a confirm dialog. The Market Tracker returns to its start value, every Player Avatar goes back to the start, and everyone gets their starting cash and shares back.

Home

The Home button takes you straight back to the start page, without an extra question.

Game over

The results window shows the winner, your learning outcomes and a short feedback form. At the end there are two buttons: Close and Start new game.
