Three boards to choose from

Classic, Wall Street or Crypto

Same board, same rules, same average tile size. What changes is how wild the swings are and what the tiles are called. The host picks one under Settings. Every set has 100 different events, so none repeats on the board.

🏛️

Wall Street

Calm and steady
Swings small
Cash DividendRate HikeTrading Halt

Small moves, never more than 3 steps of size. Patience pays.

⚖️

Classic

A bit of everything. The original Capital Clash.

🪙

Crypto & DeFi

Wild and unpredictable
Swings big
Moon RallyRug PullChain Halt

Huge wins and huge crashes. 12 events get a random plus or minus.

Fair on every board. Plus and minus tiles add up to the same total, so the market has no built-in drift. Five Skip tiles are spread across the board (none in Beginner).

Rules at a glance

TopicRule
Players3 to 10 per room
Start valuesMarket 500, money 5,000, shares 9 (host can change)
DiceLong 1 to 6, Standard 1 to 9, Short and Beginner 1 to 18. The roll is the move.
BoardsWall Street: small steady moves. Classic: balanced. Crypto: big swings. Same average size, plus = minus.
TilesPlus adds, minus subtracts, 5 Skip tiles do nothing. Tracker stays between 0 and 999.
ForecastBull or Bear moves the market 5% to 20% in small steps. About 3 in 10 change course. Bull: plus tiles +1. Bear: minus tiles -1.
BankOpens after your move. Price = Market Tracker value.
End turnLocked 5 seconds after your move; a trade unlocks it.
Total valueMoney + shares × Market Tracker value
Final roundStarts when a pawn reaches space 100. Everyone else gets one last turn.
WinnerHighest total value. Exact ties share the win.
