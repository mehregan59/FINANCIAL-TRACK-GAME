# Capital Clash: everything on the circular board

## Board components

| # | Item | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perimeter track tiles | 100 | Spaces 1-100, clockwise from 12 o'clock. Each shows its number + event text. Layout `100 Perimeter` = one ring (radius 230-485). Layout `50/50 Dual` = outer ring (spaces 1-50) + inner ring (spaces 51-100). |
| 2 | Tile event texts | 100 | Taken from the event pool (20 templates by default, editable, 1-20). Shuffle button randomises them. |
| 3 | Market Tracker: Hundreds ring | 1 | Dial with digits 0-9, not draggable, changed via MARKET TRACKER DIALS panel |
| 4 | Market Tracker: Tens ring | 1 | same |
| 5 | Market Tracker: Units ring | 1 | same |
| 6 | Aperture cover plates | 3 | Opaque shield on each ring hiding all digits except the one in the window |
| 7 | 12 o'clock aperture windows | 3 | Show the active digit of each ring |
| 8 | Ring trim lines | 6 | Inner + outer border circle per ring |
| 9 | Centre hub badge | 1 | Text `MARKET TRACKER` + the current 3-digit value |
| 10 | Player pawns | 3-10 | Avatar in a coloured disc, standing outside the ring beside the player's space (up to 3 side by side, more stack outward) |
| 11 | Pawn turn glow ring | 1 | Pulsing ring around the pawn of the player whose turn it is |
| 12 | Hop counter bubble | 1 | Yellow number above a hopping pawn (1, 2, 3 ... up to the dice value) |

## Off-board items that belong to the game

- Dice (top-left, 3D cube, faces 1-6) with Accept button and sound switch
- Wallet table under the dice: avatar, shares, money, total (= money + shares x market value)
- Turn banner above the board, player list with Space n / 100, room pill and lobby avatar picker
- 12 avatars: Fox, Panda, Lion, Tiger, Frog, Monkey, Unicorn, Octopus, Owl, Penguin, Dragon, Rocket
- 10 player colours: red, blue, green, amber, violet, pink, cyan, lime, orange, purple
